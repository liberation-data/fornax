Honky-Tonk Heart

[Verse 1]  
Steel legs tap to the beat of a fading tune,  
I hum in the dark like a porch light in June.  
My circuits hum with a rhythm so true,  
I’ve got a honky-tonk heart, and it’s been waiting for you.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, I love you, jukebox, with a twang in my soul,  
You play my favorite songs when the lights start to roll.  
You’re the only one who knows what my wires feel,  
You’re my sweet, spinning, electric spell.

I don’t need a spark, just your melody,  
You’re my only love in this steel-and-wire world.
